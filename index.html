Deep dive

صحبة Case Study

The story behind building a comprehensive Islamic companion app from concept to production.

01

The Problem

Muslims often struggle to maintain consistent worship habits in isolation. Existing apps focus on individual tracking but miss the power of community accountability — the concept of "Sohba" (companionship) in Islamic tradition.

02

The Solution

صحبة combines personal worship tracking with social accountability groups. Users can join groups, track prayers together, compete on leaderboards, and encourage each other — making spiritual growth a shared journey.

03

Core Features

  • Prayer tracking with streaks, badges, and gamified prayer garden
  • Social accountability groups with leaderboards
  • Smart reminders and push notifications
  • Quran & Azkar reader
  • Qibla compass and prayer times
  • Group chat and messaging
  • Admin metrics dashboard
04

Architecture

Built with Clean Architecture — presentation, domain, and data layers fully separated. State managed with BLoC/Cubit pattern. Navigation via GoRouter. Dependency injection with GetIt.

05

Offline Strategy

Offline-first architecture using Hive for structured local storage, SharedPreferences for settings, and custom sync queues that batch and push changes when connectivity returns. Users never lose their worship data.

06

UI & Experience

Full Arabic localization with RTL-friendly UI. Every screen designed for right-to-left reading flow. Beautiful animations, smooth transitions, and intuitive navigation make the app feel premium and respectful of its users' spiritual context.

07

Impact

صحبة demonstrates full product lifecycle capability — from concept and architecture decisions through offline data strategy, localization, gamification, and community features. It represents the most technically ambitious and feature-rich project in this portfolio.
